See every Release of a Project beside the one you chose, with who published it and what it changed.

**Releases is a list beside the chosen Release.** Each Release stands in a ruled row of its own, newest first, with its version, the day it went out, who published it and its bump as a badge saying Major, Minor or Patch, so none of them is read by its color alone. The Release the Project is at carries the one highlighted marker and every other row the gray one. Until now the page listed a version and a date in a card and said nothing about who published it or what it held.

**The chosen Release says what it published.** Its own pane names it, says that it was the whole Project and who released it when, then the version it moved from and to with its bump, one sentence on what made it that bump, and the Entries and Assets it added, changed and deleted, each kind on a row with its count. Open in History takes you to the Release among the changes it published. All of it comes from elek.io Core, which reports a Release that went out in the same shape as one that is about to, so the Release dialog and this pane say it the same way.

**Create Release stands at the head of the list**, and in the empty state while the Project has published nothing, so one button leads to a Release wherever you are on the screen.

**The Release dialog lists what would go out.** It says "Desktop sets the version from what changed" and then what makes this Release the bump it is, and below that the Entries and Assets it would add, change and delete, one row per kind with its count. It used to list every change as a sentence of its own, which said nothing about how much of it there was. A Release that changes nothing a site reads still says so, a Project with nothing to release says that instead of a version, and a refusal still says in one sentence what stopped it and what to do next.

Nothing about your Projects changes, and nothing needs a migration.
