Sign in to elek.io Cloud, or keep working on this computer only.

The first start now asks how you want to start. "Work on this computer" needs only your name and email address, which mark your changes in the History, and every part of Desktop works without an account. elek.io Cloud is optional: sign in, or create an account, now or later from the user menu.

Under your name in the header, Desktop shows where you work: "On this computer", "elek.io Cloud", or "Sync paused" once a session with elek.io Cloud ended. Sign in again from the user menu to resume it. Signing out keeps every Project on this computer and your name and email address.

Desktop keeps your session encrypted with what your system offers: the Keychain on macOS, encryption tied to your Windows account on Windows, and a keyring such as gnome-keyring or KWallet on Linux. Without one, you sign in again after each restart. On Linux desktops Chromium does not recognize, such as niri, sway or Hyprland, Desktop now asks for the Secret Service itself.

Only one Desktop runs at a time. Opening it again brings the running window to the front.

elek.io Cloud isn't open yet. Until it is, signing in from a release says Desktop can't reach it. Confirming an email address with a code and resetting a password come with a later version.
